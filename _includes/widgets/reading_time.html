{%- comment -%} Estimated reading time of `include.content` in minutes. Chinese/Japanese text has almost no spaces, so when the average "word" is very long we count characters (~400/min) instead of words (~220/min). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _text = include.content | strip_html | strip_newlines -%} {%- assign _words = _text | number_of_words | at_least: 1 -%} {%- assign _chars = _text | size -%} {%- assign _chars_per_word = _chars | divided_by: _words -%} {%- if _chars_per_word > 12 -%} {%- assign _minutes = _chars | divided_by: 400 -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _minutes = _words | divided_by: 220 -%} {%- endif -%} {{- _minutes | at_least: 1 -}}
